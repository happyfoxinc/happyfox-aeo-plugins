# Present the investigation

Start with what the engine said. Name the engine and quote the passage that
supports the finding. For an omission, identify the recorded answer and the
buyer requirement it addressed. Explain what the evidence establishes and what
remains uncertain. Cite the dated source for any claim about product fit or
accuracy.

If the answer is accurate and no change is warranted, say why and finish there.
If a decision depends on missing evidence, identify the next check and what it
would resolve.

For each proposed change, name the target page, source, or product issue and
the evidence for acting. Say what to change and what a later observation would
show if the change helped. Flag any assumption that could change the advice.

Use the user’s requested format. When several actions compete for attention,
use a short table to show their order, supporting evidence, and how to check
the result. Include effort, dependencies, or suggested owners when they help
the reader choose.
